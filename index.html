I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Changick Kim. Before joining KAIST, I was a researcher at ETRI, where I worked on visual intelligence for intelligent transportation systems.

My research interests include understanding and anticipating human behavior from video. I build multimodal systems that combine large vision-language models with spatio-temporal networks to predict pedestrian crossing intention — a core capability for safe autonomous driving.

  • Computer Vision
  • Multimodal Learning & Vision-Language Models
  • Action Anticipation
  • Pedestrian Behavior Prediction

I am always open to discussion and collaboration on pedestrian behavior understanding and multimodal video reasoning. Feel free to reach out by email.
