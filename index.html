I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Changick Kim. Before joining KAIST, I was a researcher at ETRI (Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute), where I worked on visual intelligence for intelligent transportation systems.

My research focuses on understanding and anticipating human behavior from video. Recently I have been building multimodal systems that combine large vision-language models with conventional spatio-temporal networks to predict pedestrian crossing intention — a core capability for safe autonomous driving.
